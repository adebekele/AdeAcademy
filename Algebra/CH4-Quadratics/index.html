    Teacher Controls and Lesson Flow

    Suggested 60-minute arc

    1. 8 min: Fluency warmup, students solve independently.
    2. 12 min: Root Structure, discuss Vieta as a shortcut.
    3. 12 min: Inequalities and domain restrictions.
    4. 15 min: Function Thinking and parameter questions.
    5. 10 min: Mixed Review challenge pairs.
    6. 3 min: Exit reflection: name the hidden structure.

    Discussion prompts

    • What is the fastest useful thing to compute?
    • Where do roots, coefficients, and signs meet?
    • Can we avoid expanding everything?
    • What restrictions must be checked before solving?
