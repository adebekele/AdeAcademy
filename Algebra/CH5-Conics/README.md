# Algebra / CH5-Conics

An interactive classroom review app for Chapter 5 conics.

## What It Includes

- Parabolas: vertex form, completing the square, focus and directrix
- Problem solving with parabolas: modeling, intersections, and tangency
- Maxima and minima of quadratics
- Circles: center-radius form, completing the square, and tangent length
- Ellipses: axes, foci, and equations
- Hyperbolas: standard form, foci, asymptotes, and classification
- Chapter Summary questions
- Strategy prompts, progressive hints, answer checking, full solutions, and Main Idea Review notes
