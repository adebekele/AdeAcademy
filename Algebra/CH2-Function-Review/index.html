    Teacher Controls and Lesson Flow

    Suggested 60-minute arc

    1. 12 min: Functions Basic, including notation, domain, and range.
    2. 15 min: Graphing Functions, focusing on intercepts, shifts, and key features.
    3. 15 min: Composition, with inside-first reasoning.
    4. 15 min: Inverse Functions, including tables, equations, and restrictions.
    5. 3 min: Exit reflection: how do you know what the input is?

    Discussion prompts

    • What is the input, and what is the output?
    • What makes this relation a function?
    • What feature of the graph changed, and what stayed the same?
    • Are we doing the inside function or outside function first?
    • How can we verify that two functions undo each other?
