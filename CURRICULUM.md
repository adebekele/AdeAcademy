# AdeAcademy Curriculum Map

AdeAcademy is organized by subject first, then course, then chapter lab.

## Math

| Course | Lab | Core skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Algebra Review Studio | CH2 Function Review | Function notation, graphing, composition, inverse functions |
| Algebra Review Studio | CH3 Complex Numbers | Complex arithmetic, real and imaginary parts, complex plane |
| Algebra Review Studio | CH4 Quadratics | Roots, inequalities, graph features, mixed review |
| Algebra Review Studio | CH5 Conics | Parabolas, extrema, circles, ellipses, hyperbolas |

## Physics

| Course | Lab | Core skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| College Physics Studio | CH1 Measurement, Math Tools, and Vectors | Standards, matter, dimensional analysis, uncertainty, significant figures, unit conversions, estimates, trigonometry, vectors |
| College Physics Studio | CH2 Kinematics | Motion graphs, constant acceleration, free fall, projectiles |
| College Physics Studio | CH3 Forces | Free-body diagrams, Newton's laws, friction, tension, inclines |
| College Physics Studio | CH4 Energy | Work, kinetic energy, potential energy, conservation, power |
| College Physics Studio | CH5 Momentum | Momentum, impulse, conservation, collisions, recoil |

## Design Pattern

Every lab should include:

1. Section modes.
2. Strategy choices.
3. A student answer field.
4. Check, hint, solution, and next controls.
5. Feedback, hint trail, and main-idea review.
6. Teacher controls and suggested lesson flow.
