    Teacher Controls and Lesson Flow

    Suggested 60-minute arc

    1. 15 min: Arithmetic of complex numbers in standard form.
    2. 12 min: Complex plane as coordinates (a,b).
    3. 12 min: Real and imaginary parts, including equations from matching parts.
    4. 16 min: Graphing in the complex plane, distance, and point movement.
    5. 5 min: Exit reflection: how does a+bi connect algebra to geometry?

    Discussion prompts

    • Is the answer in standard form a+bi?
    • What point represents this complex number?
    • Which part is real, and which coefficient is imaginary?
    • How did the point move in the complex plane?
