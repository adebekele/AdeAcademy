    Teacher Controls and Lesson Flow

    Suggested 75-minute arc

    1. 10 min: Standards of length, mass, time, and matter as atoms.
    2. 12 min: Dimensional analysis as a formula-checking habit.
    3. 12 min: Uncertainty, measurement limits, and significant figures.
    4. 14 min: Unit conversions, prefixes, estimates, and order of magnitude.
    5. 20 min: Coordinate systems, trigonometry, vectors, and components.
    6. 7 min: Problem-solving strategy reflection.

    Discussion prompts

    • What physical quantity is being measured?
    • What units must the final answer have?
    • How many digits are actually justified by the measurement?
    • Which triangle side is opposite or adjacent to the angle?
    • Can the answer be checked by dimensions, estimate, or vector direction?
